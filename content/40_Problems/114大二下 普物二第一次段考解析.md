# 國立屏東大學 114學年度第二學期 應用物理系
## 普通物理學 (II) 第一次段考解析 (1st Exam)

### 1. 電偶極 (Electric Dipole) (15%)
**題目：**
Point charges $q_1 = -4.2 \text{ nC}$ and $q_2 = +4.2 \text{ nC}$ are separated by a distance of $3.5 \text{ mm}$, forming an electric dipole.
(a) Find the magnitude and direction of the electric dipole moment.
(b) The charges are in a uniform electric field whose direction makes an angle of $36.8^\circ$ with the line connecting the charges. What is the magnitude of this field if the torque exerted on the dipole has magnitude $7.5 \times 10^{-9} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$?

**解析：**
(a) 電偶極矩大小 $\vec{p} = q\vec{d}$：
$$p = qd = (4.2 \times 10^{-9} \text{ C}) \times (3.5 \times 10^{-3} \text{ m}) = 1.47 \times 10^{-11} \text{ C}\cdot\text{m}$$
方向：由負電荷 $q_1$ 指向正電荷 $q_2$。

(b) 力矩公式 $\tau = pE \sin\theta$：
已知 $\tau = 7.5 \times 10^{-9} \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$, $\theta = 36.8^\circ$ (注意 $\sin 36.8^\circ \approx 0.6$)
$$E = \frac{\tau}{p \sin\theta} = \frac{7.5 \times 10^{-9}}{1.47 \times 10^{-11} \times 0.6} \approx 850.34 \text{ N/C}$$

### 2. 無限長直導線電場 (Electric Field of a Long Wire) (15%)
**題目：**
A very long, straight wire has charge per unit length $1.47 \times 10^{-10} \text{ C/m}$. At what distance from the wire is the electric-field magnitude equal to $2.52 \text{ N/C}$?

**解析：**
無限長直導線電場公式：$E = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0 r} = \frac{2k\lambda}{r}$
其中 $k \approx 8.99 \times 10^9 \text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$
$$r = \frac{2k\lambda}{E} = \frac{2 \times 8.99 \times 10^9 \times 1.47 \times 10^{-10}}{2.52}$$
$$r = \frac{2.64306}{2.52} \approx 1.049 \text{ m}$$

### 3. 環狀電荷 (Ring Charge) (15%)
**題目：**
A ring-shaped conductor with radius $a = 2.5 \text{ cm}$ has a total positive charge $Q = +0.125 \text{ nC}$ uniformly distributed around it.
(a) What is the electric field (magnitude and direction) at point P, which is on the x-axis at $x = 40 \text{ cm}$?
(b) A point charge $q = -2.5 \mu\text{C}$ is placed at P. What are the magnitude and direction of the force exerted by the charge $q$ on the ring?

**解析：**
(a) 環電荷軸線上電場公式：$E_x = \frac{kQx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}$
代入數值 ($a=0.025 \text{ m}, x=0.4 \text{ m}, Q=0.125 \times 10^{-9} \text{ C}$):
$$E_x = \frac{8.99 \times 10^9 \times 0.125 \times 10^{-9} \times 0.4}{(0.4^2 + 0.025^2)^{1.5}} = \frac{0.4495}{(0.160625)^{1.5}} \approx \frac{0.4495}{0.06437} \approx 6.98 \text{ N/C}$$
方向：沿著 +x 軸方向（遠離圓心）。

(b) 根據牛頓第三運動定律，電荷 $q$ 對環的作用力與環對 $q$ 的作用力大小相等、方向相反。
$$F = |q| E_x = (2.5 \times 10^{-6} \text{ C}) \times 6.98 \text{ N/C} \approx 1.745 \times 10^{-5} \text{ N}$$
方向：因為 $q$ 為負電，環對 $q$ 的力是向左（引力），所以 $q$ 對環的力是向右（+x 軸方向）。

### 4. 高斯定律應用：同心球體 (Gauss's Law: Concentric Spheres) (15%)
**題目：**
Solid plastic sphere ($R_1 = 8 \text{ cm}$) concentric with aluminum shell ($R_2 = 14 \text{ cm}, R_3 = 17 \text{ cm}$).
- At $r = 34 \text{ cm}$, $E = 1.7 \times 10^3 \text{ N/C}$ (radially outward).
- At $r = 12 \text{ cm}$, $E = 9.1 \times 10^4 \text{ N/C}$ (radially inward).
(a) Net charge on plastic sphere? (b) Net charge on aluminum shell? (c) Charges on inner and outer surfaces of the shell?

**解析：**
(a) 利用高斯定律 $Q_{encl} = 4\pi\epsilon_0 r^2 E = \frac{r^2 E}{k}$
在 $r=12 \text{ cm} = 0.12 \text{ m}$ 處（此時只包圍塑膠球）：
$$Q_{plastic} = \frac{(0.12)^2 \times (-9.1 \times 10^4)}{8.99 \times 10^9} \approx -1.458 \times 10^{-7} \text{ C} \approx -145.8 \text{ nC}$$

(b) 在 $r=34 \text{ cm} = 0.34 \text{ m}$ 處（包圍全部）：
$$Q_{total} = \frac{(0.34)^2 \times (1.7 \times 10^3)}{8.99 \times 10^9} \approx 2.185 \times 10^{-8} \text{ C} \approx 21.85 \text{ nC}$$
鋁殼淨電荷 $Q_{shell} = Q_{total} - Q_{plastic} = 21.85 - (-145.8) = 167.65 \text{ nC}$。

(c) 導體內部電場為零：
內表面電荷 $Q_{inner} = -Q_{plastic} = +145.8 \text{ nC}$。
外表面電荷 $Q_{outer} = Q_{shell} - Q_{inner} = 167.65 - 145.8 = 21.85 \text{ nC}$。

### 5. 絕緣球體電位 (Potential of an Insulating Sphere) (15%)
**題目：**
Charge $Q = 6 \mu\text{C}$ uniformly distributed over volume of sphere $R = 3 \text{ cm}$.
(a) Derive $V(r)$ inside. (b) $V$ at center? (c) $V$ on surface?

**解析：**
(a) 球內電場 $E(r) = \frac{kQr}{R^3}$。
電位差 $V(r) - V(R) = -\int_R^r E dr' = \int_r^R \frac{kQr'}{R^3} dr' = \frac{kQ}{2R^3}(R^2 - r^2)$。
已知 $V(R) = \frac{kQ}{R}$，故：
$$V(r) = \frac{kQ}{R} + \frac{kQ(R^2-r^2)}{2R^3} = \frac{kQ}{2R^3}(3R^2 - r^2)$$

(b) 球心 ($r=0$):
$$V(0) = \frac{3kQ}{2R} = 1.5 \times \frac{8.99 \times 10^9 \times 6 \times 10^{-6}}{0.03} \approx 2.697 \times 10^6 \text{ V}$$

(c) 表面 ($r=R$):
$$V(R) = \frac{kQ}{R} = \frac{8.99 \times 10^9 \times 6 \times 10^{-6}}{0.03} \approx 1.798 \times 10^6 \text{ V}$$

### 6. 電、細桿電位 (Potential of a Charged Rod) (15%)
**題目：**
Uniformly charged rod of length $a$, total charge $Q$. Find potential at point P, distance $x$ to the right of the rod.

**解析：**
設桿位於 $z=0$ 到 $z=a$，點 P 位於 $z = a+x$。
電荷密度 $\lambda = Q/a$，微元電荷 $dq = \lambda dz$。
$$V = \int_0^a \frac{k dq}{r} = \int_0^a \frac{k (Q/a) dz}{(a+x) - z}$$
令 $u = a+x-z$，$du = -dz$：
$$V = \frac{kQ}{a} \int_x^{a+x} \frac{du}{u} = \frac{kQ}{a} [\ln u]_x^{a+x} = \frac{kQ}{a} \ln \left( \frac{a+x}{x} \right) = \frac{kQ}{a} \ln \left( 1 + \frac{a}{x} \right)$$

### 7. 平行帶電板 (Parallel Conducting Plates) (15%)
**題目：**
Parallel plates, $d = 2.2 \text{ cm}$, $\sigma = 47 \text{ nC/m}^2$.
(a) Magnitude of $E$ between plates? (b) Potential difference $V$? (c) If $d$ is doubled, what happens to $E$ and $V$?

**解析：**
(a) 兩板間電場 $E = \frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$：
$$E = \frac{47 \times 10^{-9} \text{ C/m}^2}{8.854 \times 10^{-12} \text{ C}^2/\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2} \approx 5308.3 \text{ V/m}$$

(b) 電位差 $V = Ed$：
$$V = 5308.3 \times 0.022 \text{ m} \approx 116.78 \text{ V}$$

(c) 如果間距 $d$ 加倍：
- **電場 $E$：** 在無限大平面假設下，電場與距離無關，故 **不變**。
- **電位差 $V$：** $V = Ed$，當 $d$ 加倍時，$V$ 會 **加倍**（變為約 $233.56 \text{ V}$）。
