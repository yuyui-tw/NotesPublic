# intro
$$\displaylines{
y=f(x),x \xrightarrow{f} y\\
inverse:\ x=f^{-}(y),y \xrightarrow{f^{-}} x
}$$

ex.
$$\displaylines{
find\ inverse\ of y=f(x)=\frac{1}{2}x+1\\
sol:\\
idea:y=ax+b\to x=cy+d\\
\therefore f^{-}(x)=2x-1
}$$

# def
f is one to one (1-1) and **onto**^[滿射：指對應域等於值域的映射。即：對對應域中任意元素，都存在至少一個定義域中的元素與之對應]
$if\ x_{1}\ne x_{2}\to f(x_{1})\ne f(x_{2})$
- 較易檢驗$x_{1}、x_{2}$關係
- if f is 1-1:
	- domain D
	- range $R=f(D)=\{f(x):x\in D\}$
	then $f^{-}:R\to D\ exist$
	- 若非1-1，則需限定條件
	ex. $y=x^{2}，x\geq 0\ has\ inverse\ function$、[[Inverse Trigonometric function]](週期性函數等等)
- $f\cdot f^{-}=x$ ([[Functions#Compositions 函數合成]])
- [[Exponential and Logarithm functions]]的函數圖形對稱於$x=y$，互為反函數
