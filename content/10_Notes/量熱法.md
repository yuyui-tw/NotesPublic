# 概要

量熱法 (Calorimetry) 是一門透過測量溫度變化，來定量計算化學或物理過程中熱量 ($q$) 轉移的實驗科學。透過在量熱器 (Calorimeter) 中進行受控實驗，可以直接測得如焓變 ($\Delta H$) 和內能變化 ($\Delta U$) 等關鍵的熱力學函數。

# 核心概念

- **能量守恆**：量熱法的核心原理源於[[熱力學第一定律]]。在一個孤立系統中，反應所釋放的熱量 ($q_{rxn}$) 必須等於周遭環境（量熱器與其內容物）所吸收的熱量 ($q_{cal}$)，反之亦然。因此，$q_{rxn} + q_{cal} = 0$ 或 $q_{rxn} = -q_{cal}$。
- **熱量計算**：熱量的計算基於物質的比熱 ($s$) 或熱容 ($C$)，公式為 `q = s·m·ΔT` 或 `q = C·ΔT`。
- **定壓 vs. 定容**：實驗是在「恆定壓力」還是在「恆定體積」下進行，決定了測得的熱量 ($q$) 等同於哪個狀態函數的變化。

# 結構/要素

### 1. 基本公式

-   **比熱 (Specific Heat, $s$)**: 升高 **1 克** 物質溫度 $1^\circ\text{C}$ 所需的熱量 ($\text{J}/\text{g} \cdot {}^\circ\text{C}$)。
    $$ q = s \cdot m \cdot \Delta T $$
![[Chemistry ch06 ppt.pdf#page=27&rect=5,10,174,298|Chemistry ch06 ppt, p.27|150]]
>==水的比熱是4.184==!!
-   **熱容 (Heat Capacity, $C$)**: 升高**給定數量**的物質溫度 $1^\circ\text{C}$ 所需的熱量 ($\text{J}/^\circ\text{C}$)。
    $$ q = C \cdot \Delta T $$
    -   $q$: 熱量
    -   $m$: 質量
    -   $\Delta T$: 溫度變化 ($T_{final} - T_{initial}$)

### 2. 量熱法類型與測量目標

| 類型        | 測量條件       | 直接測量值 | 熱力學關聯            | 常用裝置                   |
| :-------- | :--------- | :---- | :--------------- | :--------------------- |
| **定壓量熱法** | 恆定壓力 ($P$) | $q_p$ | $q_p = \Delta H$ | 咖啡杯式量熱器                |
| **定容量熱法** | 恆定體積 ($V$) | $q_v$ | $q_v = \Delta U$ | 彈卡計 (Bomb Calorimeter) |

> [!NOTE] 彈卡計例題
> A quantity of 1.435 g of naphthalene ($C_{10}H_{8}$), a pungent-smelling substance used in moth repellents, was burned in a constantvolume bomb calorimeter. Consequently, the temperature of the water rose from 20.28° C to 25.95° C. If the heat capacity of the bomb plus water was 10.17 kJ/℃, calculate the heat of combustion of naphthalene on a molar basis; that is, find the molar heat of combustion.
>
> **Solution**
> 
> **1. Calculate heat absorbed by the calorimeter ($q_{cal}$):**
> 首先，計算量熱計本身與其中的水所吸收的總熱量。
> -   Heat capacity of calorimeter ($C_{cal}$) = $10.17 \, \text{kJ}/^\circ\text{C}$
> -   Temperature change ($\Delta T$) = $T_{final} - T_{initial} = 25.95^\circ\text{C} - 20.28^\circ\text{C} = 5.67^\circ\text{C}$
> 
> $$ q_{cal} = C_{cal} \times \Delta T = (10.17 \, \text{kJ}/^\circ\text{C}) \times (5.67^\circ\text{C}) = 57.6639 \, \text{kJ} $$
> 
> **2. Determine the heat of reaction ($q_{rxn}$):**
> 根據[[熱力學第一定律]]，在一個孤立系統中，反應釋放的熱量必須等於量熱計吸收的熱量，但符號相反。
> The heat released by the reaction ($q_{rxn}$) is the negative of the heat absorbed by the calorimeter.
> $$ q_{rxn} = -q_{cal} = -57.6639 \, \text{kJ} $$
> 因為這是在定容(constant volume)的彈卡計中進行，所以反應熱等於內能變化 ($q_v = \Delta U$)。
> 
> **3. Calculate molar heat of combustion:**
> 最後，將總熱量轉換為莫耳燃燒熱 (kJ/mol)。
> -   Molar mass of naphthalene (C₁₀H₈) = $10(12.011) + 8(1.008) = 128.174 \, \text{g/mol}$
> -   Moles of naphthalene = $\frac{\text{mass}}{\text{molar mass}} = \frac{1.435 \, \text{g}}{128.174 \, \text{g/mol}} = 0.0111958... \, \text{mol}$
> 
> $$ \text{Molar heat of combustion} (\Delta U) = \frac{q_{rxn}}{\text{moles}} = \frac{-57.6639 \, \text{kJ}}{0.0111958 \, \text{mol}} \approx -5150 \, \text{kJ/mol} $$
> 
> The temperature change (5.67°C) has three significant figures, so we report the final answer with three significant figures.
> **Answer:** The molar heat of combustion of naphthalene is **-5.15 x 10³ kJ/mol**.


> [!NOTE] 咖啡杯式量熱器 例題一：酸鹼中和反應熱
> A student mixes 50.0 mL of 1.0 M HCl with 50.0 mL of 1.0 M NaOH in a coffee-cup calorimeter. The initial temperature of both solutions is 21.0 °C. After the reaction, the final temperature of the mixture is 27.5 °C. Assuming the densities of the solutions are 1.0 g/mL and their specific heats are 4.18 J/g°C, and the calorimeter itself has a negligible heat capacity, calculate the enthalpy change ($\Delta H$) for the neutralization reaction in kJ/mol.
>
> **Solution**
> 
> **1. Calculate heat absorbed by the solution ($q_{sol}$):**
> 總質量 $m = (50.0 + 50.0) \, \text{mL} \times 1.0 \, \text{g/mL} = 100.0 \, \text{g}$
> 溫差 $\Delta T = 27.5^\circ\text{C} - 21.0^\circ\text{C} = 6.5^\circ\text{C}$
> $$ q_{sol} = s \cdot m \cdot \Delta T = (4.18 \, \text{J/g}^\circ\text{C}) \cdot (100.0 \, \text{g}) \cdot (6.5 \, {}^\circ\text{C}) = 2717 \, \text{J} = 2.717 \, \text{kJ} $$
> 
> **2. Determine the heat of reaction ($q_{rxn}$):**
> 因為量熱器熱容量可忽略 (negligible heat capacity)，所以反應放出的熱等於溶液吸收的熱。
> $$ q_{rxn} = -q_{sol} = -2.717 \, \text{kJ} $$
>
> **3. Calculate molar heat of reaction ($\Delta H$):**
> 反應生成水的莫耳數 $n = 1.0 \, \text{mol/L} \times 0.050 \, \text{L} = 0.050 \, \text{mol}$
> $$ \Delta H = \frac{q_{rxn}}{n} = \frac{-2.717 \, \text{kJ}}{0.050 \, \text{mol}} \approx -54.3 \, \text{kJ/mol} $$
>
> **Answer:** The enthalpy change for the neutralization reaction is **-54.3 kJ/mol**.


> [!NOTE] 咖啡杯式量熱器 例題二：金屬比熱的測定
> A 75.0 g piece of an unknown metal at 100.0 °C is placed into a coffee-cup calorimeter containing 150.0 g of water at 20.0 °C. The final temperature of the water and the metal is 23.4 °C. Assuming the calorimeter has negligible heat capacity, what is the specific heat of the unknown metal?
>
> **Solution**
>
> **1. Calculate heat gained by water ($q_{water}$):**
> 水吸收的熱量：
> $$ q_{water} = s_{water} \cdot m_{water} \cdot \Delta T_{water} = (4.18 \, \text{J/g}^\circ\text{C}) \cdot (150.0 \, \text{g}) \cdot (23.4 - 20.0)^\circ\text{C} = 2131.8 \, \text{J} $$
>
> **2. Relate heat lost by metal ($q_{metal}$) to heat gained by water:**
> 根據能量守恆，金屬放出的熱等於水吸收的熱。
> $$ q_{metal} = -q_{water} = -2131.8 \, \text{J} $$
>
> **3. Calculate the specific heat of the metal ($s_{metal}$):**
> 從 $q_{metal} = s_{metal} \cdot m_{metal} \cdot \Delta T_{metal}$ 計算比熱：
> $$ s_{metal} = \frac{q_{metal}}{m_{metal} \cdot \Delta T_{metal}} = \frac{-2131.8 \, \text{J}}{(75.0 \, \text{g}) \cdot (23.4 - 100.0)^\circ\text{C}} = \frac{-2131.8 \, \text{J}}{-5745 \, \text{g}^\circ\text{C}} \approx 0.371 \, \text{J/g}^\circ\text{C} $$
>
> **Answer:** The specific heat capacity of the unknown metal is **0.371 J/g°C**.

# 原理/推導

量熱法之所以是連結理論與實踐的橋樑，是因為它將可直接測量的「熱量」，與抽象的「狀態函數」變化聯繫起來。

-   **定壓條件 ($q_p = \Delta H$)**: 
    如在 [[焓]] 筆記中所推導，恆壓下的熱量交換 $q_p$ 等於系統的焓變化 $\Delta H$。因此，使用咖啡杯式量熱器測得的熱量，直接對應反應的焓變。

-   **定容條件 ($q_v = \Delta U$)**: 
    根據[[熱力學第一定律]]，$\Delta U = q + w$。在恆定體積下，系統體積不變 ($\Delta V = 0$)，因此系統不做壓力-體積功 ($w = -P\Delta V = 0$)。此時，第一定律簡化為：
    $$ \Delta U = q_v $$
    因此，使用彈卡計在恆容下測得的熱量，直接對應反應的內能變化。

-   **$\Delta H$ 與 $\Delta U$ 的轉換**: 
    兩種量熱法測得的結果可以透過 `ΔH = ΔU + RTΔn` (針對理想氣體反應) 的關係式進行換算，這為實驗數據的應用提供了極大的靈活性。
