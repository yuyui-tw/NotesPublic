# 概要
光是一種電磁輻射 (electromagnetic radiation)，在古典物理學中被描述為一種**電磁波 (electromagnetic wave)**。它由相互垂直震盪的電場 (electric field) 與磁場 (magnetic field) 組成，並以光速在真空中傳播。光的顏色與能量由其波的特性（如頻率和波長）決定。

# 核心概念
- **電磁波 (Electromagnetic Wave)**
	- 光的本質，是一種不需介質即可傳播的橫波 (transverse wave)。
	- It consists of an electric field and a magnetic field oscillating in phase, perpendicular to each other and to the direction of wave propagation.
- **波長 (Wavelength, $\lambda$)**
	- 波上兩個連續相同相位點之間的距離，例如兩個相鄰波峰 (crests) 或波谷 (troughs) 的距離。
	- Unit: meter (m) or nanometer (nm).
- **頻率 (Frequency, $\nu$)**
	- 在一秒鐘內，通過空間中某一點的波的數量 (The number of waves that pass through a point in space per second)。
	- Unit: Hertz (Hz), or $s^{-1}$.
- **波速 (Wave Speed, $c$)**
	- The speed of an electromagnetic wave in a vacuum.
	- $c \approx 3.00 \times 10^8$ m/s.

# 結構/要素
- **電磁波關係式 (Wave Equation)**
	- 波速、波長與頻率三者之間存在固定關係。波長越長，頻率越低；反之亦然。
	- The speed of light ($c$) is the product of its wavelength ($\lambda$) and frequency ($\nu$).
	$$c = \lambda \nu$$
- **電磁波譜 (Electromagnetic Spectrum)**
	- The range of all types of electromagnetic radiation, ordered by wavelength or frequency.
	- 依序為 (from long wavelength to short):
		1. 無線電波 (Radio waves)
		2. 微波 (Microwaves)
		3. 紅外線 (Infrared, IR)
		4. **可見光 (Visible light)**
		5. 紫外線 (Ultraviolet, UV)
		6. X射線 (X-rays)
		7. 伽馬射線 (Gamma rays)

# 原理/推導
光的波動理論源於19世紀的物理學家馬克士威 (James Clerk Maxwell)。他在1873年提出的馬克士威方程式 (Maxwell's equations) 成功地統一了電學、磁學和光學，並從理論上預測了電磁波的存在。

Maxwell's theory states that a changing electric field produces a magnetic field, and a changing magnetic field produces an electric field. This interplay creates a self-propagating electromagnetic wave. The calculated speed of this wave in a vacuum was nearly identical to the measured speed of light, leading to the conclusion that **light is an electromagnetic wave**.
