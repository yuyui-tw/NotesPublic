# 概要
普朗克量子理論 (Planck's Quantum Theory) 是物理學家馬克斯·普朗克 (Max Planck) 於1900年提出的革命性理論，被視為量子力學的開端 (the birth of quantum mechanics)。為了解決古典物理學無法解釋的「黑體輻射」問題，普朗克假設能量的發射與吸收並非連續，而是以不連續的「量子」形式進行。

(Planck proposed that energy is radiated or absorbed in discrete units, or "quanta," rather than continuously.)

# 核心概念
- **能量的量子化 (Quantization of Energy)**
	- 該理論的核心思想。原子或分子等振子 (oscillators) 發射或吸收的電磁輻射能量，只能是某個最小能量單位的整數倍。
	- This idea was a radical departure from classical physics, which held that energy could be varied in any arbitrary, continuous amount.
- **能量量子 (Quantum of Energy)**
	- 普朗克提出的最小能量單位，其能量值與輻射的頻率成正比。
	- Any total emitted or absorbed energy must be an integer multiple of this unit (e.g., $1h\nu, 2h\nu, 3h\nu...$).

# 結構/要素
- **普朗克公式 (Planck's Equation)**
	- 定義了單一能量量子 ($E$) 與其對應電磁波頻率 ($\nu$) 之間的關係。
	- The energy ($E$) of a single quantum is proportional to its frequency ($\nu$).
	$$E = h\nu$$
- **普朗克常數 (Planck's Constant, $h$)**
	- 公式中的比例常數，是一個基本的物理常數 (a fundamental physical constant)。
	- $h \approx 6.626 \times 10^{-34} \text{ J} \cdot \text{s}$. Its small value explains why quantum effects are not noticeable in the macroscopic world.

# 原理/推導
- **背景：黑體輻射 (Black-Body Radiation)**
	- **黑體 (black body)** 是一個理想化的物體，能吸收所有入射的電磁輻射。受熱時，它會發射出具有**特定**頻譜分佈的熱輻射。
	- Classical physics (specifically the Rayleigh-Jeans law) failed to explain the observed spectrum, predicting that the energy emitted at shorter wavelengths would approach infinity. This contradiction is known as the **紫外災變 (Ultraviolet Catastrophe)**.
- **普朗克的解決方案 (Planck's Solution)**
	- 為了解決這個矛盾，普朗克假設構成黑體的振子，其能量不能連續變化，只能處於一系列不連續的、量子化的能階 (quantized energy levels) 上。
	- An oscillator emits a quantum of energy, $E=h\nu$, only when it makes a transition from a higher energy level to a lower one.
	- This assumption correctly predicted the experimental black-body radiation curves by suppressing high-frequency emissions, thus launching the quantum era.
